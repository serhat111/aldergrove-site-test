Privacy

Your computer. Your data.

Aldergrove has no account and no server of its own. With a local model, your chats are written and answered on your computer, and we never see them. Some features do reach the internet when you use them; this page lists every one, what it sends, and to whom.

Stays on your Mac
Chats and replies, memory and the notebook, files you add, meeting audio and transcripts, voice, documents, the model itself, and your sign-ins.
Leaves only when you use it
Web search, pages and Wikipedia online, maps and photos, your connected apps, and a cloud model if you choose one.

01

What stays on your computer

Your chats
With a local model, every message and reply is made on your Mac and saved there. Temporary chats aren't saved at all.
What it remembers
Memory, your profile and the nightly notebook are files on your Mac. You can read and edit them in Aldergrove, and "forget" removes a memory.
Your files
Aldergrove only reads folders you add to a chat. It moves files to the Bin rather than deleting them.
Meetings and voice
Transcripts and speech are made on your Mac. Audio isn't sent anywhere.
Sign-ins
Mail and calendar sign-ins are kept in the Mac's keychain. Code mode never sees them.

02

What leaves, and only when you use it

These features need the internet. Each one sends only what it needs, and only when you or your request uses it.

Want none of it? Download a Wikipedia pack and Aldergrove answers from it with no internet at all. Turn off Wi-Fi and everything on your Mac keeps working.

03

Cloud models are your choice

Aldergrove works without any cloud model. If you add one with your own key, chats that use it go to that provider under their terms, and the chat says so: the status line changes from "Running on your Mac" to "Cloud model". We never call a chat private if it used a cloud model.

04

What we never do

  • No account. There's nothing to sign up for to use Aldergrove.
  • No analytics or tracking in the app. It doesn't report what you do, which features you use, or how often.
  • No ads, and no selling data.
  • No training on what you do. Your chats and files are never used to train a model.
  • No passwords typed for you. In Chrome, Aldergrove never types passwords or card numbers.
  • Nothing final without you. Sending, posting, booking and deleting wait for you to press the button.

05

Error reports and updates

Error reports are off
When something breaks, the error stays on your Mac. You can read the exact report and send it yourself. It has no chat text or file contents. If you turn on automatic reports, they're sent without asking.
Updates wait for a click
Aldergrove checks for an update when you press Check, and downloads when you press Download.
Sharing with other devices is off
The phone page and the local API for other programs stay off until you turn them on, and only work on your own network.

06

This website

This site has no tracking or analytics. If you join the beta list, your email and your Mac's memory size go to Formspree, which collects the form for us. We use them only to email you about the beta, and you can ask to be removed at any time.
