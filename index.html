Product

Two modes. One model that checks its own work.

Chat

Questions, errands and documents, with answers you can check.

  • Web search, Wikipedia and page reading, with sources
  • Offline Wikipedia and maps when there's no internet
  • Remembers what you tell it, and tells you it did
  • Shows its plan first for anything with several steps
  • Reads PDFs, Office files, images and code
  • Reminders, files and folders, a calculator that doesn't guess

Code

Builds and fixes software in your project folder, and proves it works.

  • Plans, writes the code, runs the tests, retries until they pass
  • "Done" only when the plan's own tests pass
  • Looks at the pages it builds, at desktop and phone width
  • Finds unreadable text, overflow, broken images and dead links
  • Matches a design you point it at
  • Every task can be undone, all at once or file by file

Page check

It looks at what it built.

After a frontend change, Aldergrove renders the page out of sight, takes screenshots and measures them. Each problem it finds is boxed where it is on the page and sent back to be fixed.

Privacy

Your computer. Your data.

  • Runs locally. With a local model, your chats, files and memory stay on your computer.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, and it works offline.
  • No ads, no selling data, no training on what you do.
  • Sign-ins in your keychain. Mail and calendar access stays in the Mac's keychain.
  • Reports only when you say so. Error reports are off by default, and you read the exact text first.
  • Cloud is your choice. Bring your own keys if you want a cloud model; the chat says what it costs.
